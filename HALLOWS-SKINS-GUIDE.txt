VOIDRUNNER — ALL HALLOWS · THE WARDROBE
The prize skins' toolkit: every hook, what it is handed, and when.
Written 28 Sep 2026. Prepatch only; never deployed.


================================================================================
0. WHAT IT IS
================================================================================

The vigil's four prizes (DEV_SKINS 'hl-*') are event cosmetics, and event
cosmetics carry the event's budget (user, 28 Sep: "cosmetics for events have
a much higher budget"; "forget latency just keep it reasonable"). So they do
not go through the ordinary awards' one pass over a finished hull. They are
drawn on THE WARDROBE, a toolkit built on THE KIT for these skins only:

  THE WEARER   one object every hook is handed, called `me`. The art reads the
               pilot through it and never through P: where it is, how fast,
               turning which way, hurt, dashing, low, how hot the fight is. It
               also asks it for things: sockets on the hull, moments,
               particles, rope and cloth, springs, trail points, lamps, lights.
  THE HOOKS    35 of them. Draw hooks for every layer of the pilot, and Fx
               hooks for every moment of a run a skin can answer.
  THE STAGE    a puppet that flies, fires, dashes, kills and levels by
               itself, drawn through exactly the same hooks. It is the skin
               menu's showcase, the dropdown's hover card and the bench.

The same art draws in a run, in the menu and in the design file without a
change, because none of it reads the game directly.

THE PRIZES (planning sheet §11; the logic that grants them is unchanged)
  150    hl-carved    THE CARVED     fx 'hallows' tier 1  a face cut into the
                                     hull, nothing lit behind it yet
  400    hl-lantern   THE LANTERN    fx 'hallows' tier 2  lit from inside;
                                     rounds go out as sparks
  750    hl-hallows   ALL HALLOWS    fx 'hallows' tier 3  burning; kills come
                                     apart as embers
  1,200  hl-lostsoul  THE LOST SOUL  fx 'lostsoul'        the ordinary hull as
                                     a ghost, flickering at 4%
  One skin line, carved to burning, plus one on top. me.fx and me.tier tell
  the hooks which is worn, so one set of hooks can draw all four (the
  imperial line's pattern), or branch as far as they like.

WHERE THINGS STAND
  The toolkit, the game's wiring, the menu stage, the admin SKIN LAB and the
  bench were built and tested on 28 Sep. The user's art is in the same day:
  all 35 hooks, lifted as HL_ART_VER e69cd08f81 (prepatch only). The 27 Sep
  prize hooks are gone from hl-art.js. The rind, lid, vine and carved face
  burn up the line from THE CARVED to ALL HALLOWS, and the soul is a dashed
  ghost of the runner's hull. Tested on the bench (every prize, every event,
  the lit room) and in runs, the menu stage, THE PATCH and a death, with no
  hook errors. The hand-over in 3 is now only a fallback for an art file that
  fails to load.

================================================================================

  hl-wardrobe.js     THE WARDROBE. Inline in index.html right after THE KIT,
                     byte for byte, the way hl-kit.js is. Its last part is the
                     SKINS placeholder block: every hlSkin* hook, between
                         /* ===…
                            ALL HALLOWS · SKINS — art hooks
                         …
                         /* ===================== end of ALL HALLOWS · SKINS art hooks ===================== */
  index.html         also has "ALL HALLOWS · SKINS — the adapter" right after
                     the wardrobe: the game's side, which reads the pilot into
                     a wearer and calls the passes from where the game draws.
                     It is not in hl-wardrobe.js because the harness has no
                     game to read.
  hl-art.js          THE ART, as for Act I: its function declarations replace
                     placeholders by name. Skin hooks go in the same file,
                     anywhere between its two Act I marker lines.
  hl-skin-base.js    the harness base (prepatch only): shims, then loads
                     hl-kit.js → hl-wardrobe.js → the art, then builds the bench.
  Hallows Skins.dc.html
                     the design file: the bench (prepatch only).

  PUTTING A NEW VERSION IN
      node C:\.claude\lift-hl-art.mjs --art <art.js>
          The skin hooks are checked like the Act I ones: the art may
          replace any function in either placeholder block, and nothing else.
          HL_ART_VER is stamped from the file.
      node C:\.claude\lift-hl-art.mjs --wardrobe <hl-wardrobe.js>
          Swaps the inline wardrobe (the toolkit itself). It refuses if the new
          text declares a name the rest of the page has, drops the SKINS
          markers, or stops the page parsing. Editing prepatch
          hl-wardrobe.js in place and lifting that same file works: the old
          block is found by its marker lines.

  LOADING (the 27 Sep rule, unchanged). A worn prize fetches hl-art.js whether
  the event is live or not (hlArtWanted). The art's own list of things to make
  ahead, hlSkinPreload(me), runs one job per idle moment the first time each
  prize is worn on each pilot, and again after the art changes. It runs only
  for a worn prize, never for the rest of the event.

  SHIPPING. Nothing new to carry: the wardrobe and adapter are in index.html,
  and the skins' art is in hl-art.js, which already ships. hl-wardrobe.js,
  hl-skin-base.js, the design file and this guide stay in prepatch.

================================================================================
3. THE RULES FOR SKIN ART
================================================================================

  - READ THE WEARER, NEVER THE GAME. Everything a hook needs is on `me` or is
    handed in. P, bullets and enemies are not the stage's or the bench's,
    and art that reads them will draw in the wrong place there.
  - DRAW, DO NOT DECIDE. A hook may write only me.art (its own bag), the art
    fields of what it asked for (m.art on a moment, R.art on a round), and
    the .to of its own springs. Nothing a hook does changes the run.
  - ADVANCE ONLY IN hlSkinTick. It runs once a frame wherever the prize is
    (a run, the menu stage, the bench). The draw hooks may be called more
    than once a frame or skipped. Ropes, cloth, chains and springs made
    through me.* are stepped for you after hlSkinTick. me.every(key, rate,
    fn) is the emitter, and works only inside hlSkinTick.
  - FX HOOKS START THINGS. They are called once, at the moment. Start a
    moment (me.moment), emit particles (me.emit), kick a spring. Their
    return value is ignored.
  - TIME: me.clock (the skin's own, frozen while the run is held),
    me.since.<event>, m.k / m.age on a moment, and uiTime for anything that
    should keep breathing on a paused screen.
  - LEAVE ctx AS YOU FOUND IT. The wardrobe saves and restores around every
    hook, but a hook that throws halfway through a save still leaves the
    stack uneven for that frame.
  - A HOOK THAT THROWS IS SWITCHED OFF. The error is logged once, and that
    function is skipped until the art is reloaded (a new function object).
    The game drops any frame that throws, so one bad line would otherwise
    stop the run.
  - NO BUDGET, NO PANEL GATES. The effects panel's switches (FXO.*) belong to
    the ordinary game. The wardrobe gates nothing on them, and the art does
    not have to.
  - WHAT A SKIN NEVER TAKES. Arrows are never dressed: their silhouette is
    information a zodiac pilot reads their own volley by. Bosses are never
    dressed by hlSkinBody: the bespoke ones own their silhouette. The game's
    telegraphs, pips and elite marks must still read through anything drawn
    on a body.
  - LIT ROOMS. In the vigil and the areas the kit's light pass runs after the
    pilot is drawn. So everything in hlSkinTrail, hlSkinBack, hlSkinHull,
    hlSkinFront and 'back' / 'front' moments is lit like the floor (it goes
    dark in the dark). Anything that should glow in the dark belongs in
    hlSkinGlow, a 'glow' moment, a 'glow' particle kind, or a real light
    (hlSkinLights). me.lit says which kind of room this is. THE HOUSE's
    darkness is a gameplay rule on the fog, and a skin's light does not
    lift it.
  - THE 27 SEP HOOKS. hlSkinBack, hlSkinFront, hlSkinRound, hlSkinDebris and
    hlSkinBody still call hlSkinUnder / Player / Bullet / Part / Enemy while
    they are placeholders and me.real is true (a run, the bench). Once the
    art declares the new hook, the old one is no longer called and can be
    deleted from hl-art.js.

4. THE ORDER THINGS ARE DRAWN IN
================================================================================

A FRAME IN A RUN (world space, camera applied, unless said)
    the floor, hazards, the room … bodies (hlSkinBody over each one)
    hlSkinTrail(me)                          ─┐ under the ship
    the wearer's 'lit' particles              │
    'back' moments (hlSkinMoment)            ─┘
    the ship's afterimages (hlSkinGhost each, in hull space)
    hlSkinBack(me)                           just before the hull
    hlSkinHullSwap(me)  — true: the stock hull is not drawn at all
    the stock hull (glow, plating, detail, canopy), unless swapped
    hlSkinHull(me)      in hull space, over the finished hull
    hlSkinFront(me)     over the hull
    'front' moments
      (if neither hlSkinHull nor hlSkinFront returned true: the stock rim)
    [lit rooms only: the room's light, with hlSkinLights(me) in it]
    ── additive from here ──
    enemy rounds, then the pilot's rounds (hlSkinRound each)
    beams, lances … the game's particles (hlSkinDebris each)
    the event's own glow pass
    the wearer's 'glow' particles, hlSkinGlow(me), 'glow' moments
    ── the world ends; the post (bloom) ──
    the vignette
    hlSkinScreen(me), 'screen' moments       CSS px, under the HUD
    the HUD

  Not drawn: the hull (and everything from hlSkinBack to 'front' moments)
  while dead, downed in co-op (the wreck), in FUSION CORE, or in the strike.
  me.hidden is true then. The trail, particles, glow and screen passes still
  run, so a death can play out over the empty place.

THE STAGE (menu, dropdown card, bench) draws the same passes in the same
order, inside its box: hlSkinStage(me, box) for the backdrop (true = drawn),
bodies with hlSkinBody, afterimages, under, back, swap / stock hull, hull,
front, [LIT ROOM on the bench], rounds, sparks (hlSkinDebris), glow.


================================================================================
5. THE WEARER (`me`)
================================================================================

WHO
  me.s the DEV_SKINS row · me.id · me.fx 'hallows' | 'lostsoul' · me.tier 1–3
  (0 for the soul) · me.col · me.look (what hlSkinLook returned)
  me.pilot 'runner' | 'ember' | 'hacker' | 'melee' · me.where 'run' |
  'stage' | 'bench' · me.scene 'run' | 'hub' | 'vigil' | an area id | 'stage'
  me.real (the global P is this pilot: a run, the bench) · me.live (a run is
  being played this frame) · me.lit (the room has the light pass) ·
  me.hidden (the hull is not being drawn)
  me.hullCol: the colour the game drew the hull in this frame (hurt flash,
  Ghost Step's violet), for a swapped hull to honour

WHERE AND HOW IT MOVES
  x, y, ang (the hull's heading) · vx, vy, speed, dir (heading of motion)
  turn (rad/s, smoothed) · r (P.r) · R (r + 11: the ring the ordinary
  awards draw at) · stretch { along, across } (THE KIT's hlStretch of the
  speed)
  trail: samples { x, y, ang, t, speed, dash } spaced trailGap px, kept
  look.trail s. Read it through me.trailPts().

ITS STATE
  hp, maxHp, hpK (0..1) · low (under 30%, until back over 42%) · hurt (the
  game's hurt flash, s) · phasing · blink (the i-frame flicker, on the
  frames drawPlayer skips) · dashing, dashK (0..1 through the dash),
  dashX, dashY · parry (THE VAGRANT's window is up) · still (a perfect
  parry is holding the room) · down (co-op wreck) · shield (count) · level
  · wave · kills

GIVEN FOR NOTHING (secondary motion, springs the wardrobe steps)
  lean      −1..1, banks into turns, with a little overshoot
  recoil    px, kicked backwards by every volley and springing home
  flinch    kicked by every hurt, ringing out
  bob       −1..1, fades in only after about a second of standing still
  pulse     0..1.5, +0.6 a kill, draining at 2.4/s
  heat      0..1: +0.07 a kill (elite 0.16, boss 0.45), +0.004 a round; held
            1.6 s after the last kill, then draining at 0.14/s. How hot the
            fight is, for a skin that burns brighter the longer it goes.
  streak    kills each within 2.2 s of the last · best (this run)
  firing    a round in the last 0.3 s · idle (s without moving or firing)
  lowK      0..1, eases toward low
  since     { equip, run, shot, kill, hurt, heal, shield, dash, dashEnd,
              parry, perfect, level, wave, low, down, death, streak }:
            seconds since each (99 = never)

THE ART'S OWN
  me.art (kept across runs until the prize is taken off; reset it in
  hlSkinRunFx if a run should start clean) · me.bodies, me.springs (what
  me.rope/cloth/chain/spring made) · me.moments · me.parts · me.rounds


================================================================================
6. THE HOOKS
================================================================================

THE LOOK AND THE CLOCK
hlSkinLook(me) → {}
    Called when a prize is put on (and when the art loads or reloads). What
    it returns is me.look. The wardrobe reads these keys, all optional:
      trail (s, 0.5)   trailGap (px, 3)   streakGap (s, 2.2)
      streaks ([5, 10, 20, 35, 50, 75, 100, 150, 200, 300])
      drag (1.2) / settle (60): how ropes and cloth stream (see 7)
      kinds: { name: { layer: 'lit'|'glow', make(q, o), step(q, dt),
               draw(q, me) } }: particle kinds of the skin's own, beside THE
               KIT's
    Anything else is yours (palettes per tier, say).
hlSkinPreload(me) → [jobs]
    Functions to run ahead, one per idle moment, the first time this prize
    is worn on this pilot. Make sprites here (hlWrSprite), so the first
    frame does not pay for them.
hlSkinTick(me, dt)
    Once a frame. Set springs' .to, make ropes / cloth / chains (first ask),
    emit with me.every, run the art's own timers. dt is 0 while the run is
    held.

THE LAYERS (see 4 for the order; world space unless said)
hlSkinTrail(me)                     under the ship. me.trailPts + me.ribbon.
hlSkinBack(me)                      just before the hull: cloth, chains, the
                                    glow behind it.
hlSkinHullSwap(me) → true           HULL SPACE (origin on the hull, x along
                                    the nose). The prize IS the hull: the
                                    stock glow, plating, detail and canopy are
                                    not drawn. Honour me.hullCol / me.hurt /
                                    me.phasing, which the stock hull would
                                    have shown.
hlSkinHull(me) → true if drew       HULL SPACE, over the finished hull.
                                    me.hullPath(); ctx.clip() to carve into the
                                    plating.
hlSkinFront(me) → true if drew      over the hull: halos, orbiting things,
                                    upright things (me.upright, sock 'above').
hlSkinGhost(me, g, f) → true        HULL SPACE at one of the dash
                                    afterimages: g { x, y, ang, phase (Ghost
                                    Step) }, f 0..1 (1 = the newest).
hlSkinRound(me, b, c, R) → true     one of the pilot's rounds, whole, glow and
                                    streak included. Additive. c is the colour
                                    the game would use (crit, venom, solar,
                                    deep, hacked): keep the state readable. R
                                    is the round's record (see 7).
hlSkinDebris(me, p, t, rad) → true  one of the game's own sparks (hits, kills,
                                    everything). p { x, y, r, color, … },
                                    t 0..1 of its life, rad the size the game
                                    would draw. Additive.
hlSkinBody(me, e, ea)               over each body of the roster (not
                                    bosses). ea is the body's own alpha: a
                                    phased body stays as faint as it is.
hlSkinGlow(me)                      additive, over everything in the world.
hlSkinMoment(me, m)                 each live moment, on its layer (see 7).
hlSkinScreen(me)                    CSS px (0..W, 0..H), over the world and
                                    under the HUD.
hlSkinLights(me)                    only in a lit room: me.light({ x, y, r,
                                    col, a, flick, seed, z, cone, … }) adds a
                                    real light with shadows (THE KIT's
                                    hlLight).
hlSkinStage(me, box) → true         the menu stage's backdrop, in the canvas's
                                    units (box { x, y, w, h }). False: the
                                    default dark grid.

THE MOMENTS (called once, when it happens)
hlSkinEquipFx(me)              put on: from the menu (each stage showing it)
                               or mid-run
hlSkinRunFx(me)                a run begins wearing it (reset me.art here)
hlSkinShotFx(me, b, R)         a round leaves the hull (each one)
hlSkinVolleyFx(me, n, crits)   n rounds this frame: the muzzle. (me.recoil
                               has already been kicked.)
hlSkinRoundEndFx(me, R, how)   a round is gone. how is 'hit' (it still had
                               life) or 'spent' (ran out). A room cleared of
                               rounds at once does not fire this.
hlSkinKillFx(me, e, o)         the pilot put a body down. o { x, y, r, col,
                               ang (the blow's direction), boss, elite, type,
                               sides, streak }
hlSkinStreakFx(me, n)          the streak reached n (look.streaks)
hlSkinHurtFx(me, dmg)          health went down (me.flinch is kicked)
hlSkinHealFx(me, amt)          health went up (also on a level-up's max-hp)
hlSkinShieldFx(me, n, up)      shields now n; up: one was gained
hlSkinDashFx(me, dx, dy)       a dash, along (dx, dy)
hlSkinDashEndFx(me)
hlSkinParryFx(me, perfect)     THE VAGRANT turned a blow aside
hlSkinLevelFx(me, lvl)
hlSkinWaveFx(me, n)
hlSkinLowFx(me, on)            under 30% (on) / back over 42% (off)
hlSkinDownFx(me, down)         co-op: wrecked / back up
hlSkinDeathFx(me)              the hull is gone (state 'dead'). me.hidden is
                               already true: whatever plays here plays
                               without a ship under it.


================================================================================
7. THE TOOLS
================================================================================

SOCKETS: where things attach, per hull, in hull space
  me.sock(name) → { x, y, ang } in the world · me.sockL(name) → [x, y]
  me.local(lx, ly) → a hull-space point in the world
  names: nose · muzzle · canopy · core · tail · exhaust · wingL · wingR ·
         tipL · tipR (taken off each pilot's hullPath points)
         above · below (NOT on the hull: square to the screen, R + 4 over and
         under it; for a lantern that hangs, a crown that floats)
  me.inHull(fn) runs fn in hull space · me.upright(fn) with the origin on
  the hull, axes square to the screen · me.hullPath() the outline as a path
  (in hull space: fill, stroke, clip)

MOMENTS: anything with a length
  me.moment(name, dur, o) → m. Aged by the wardrobe; drawn every frame by
  hlSkinMoment(me, m) until m.age reaches m.dur.
    o.x, o.y, o.ang  where (default: the pilot, now)
    o.follow         rides with the pilot
    o.layer          'back' (under the hull, lit) · 'front' (over the hull)
                     · 'glow' (additive) · 'screen' (CSS px). Default:
                     'front' if it follows, else 'glow'.
    o.tl             a THE KIT timeline ({ tracks, cues }); m.tl.v('track')
                     is scrubbed with the moment, so a flourish can be keyed
                     rather than written as maths. o.tl.len sets the length.
    anything else    yours, on m.o
  m { name, dur, age, k (0..1), x, y, ang, follow, layer, seed, o, art, tl }
  me.playing(name) → the live ones of that name.
  me.env(ev, dur, ease) → 1 at the event, falling to 0 over dur (a flash that
  answers every kill: me.env('kill', 0.3)).

PARTICLES: the wearer's own pool (1,800)
  me.emit(kind, x, y, n, o): THE KIT's kinds (ember, smoke, mote, clod,
  wisp, bat) and any in look.kinds. o is the kind's own (col, size, life,
  vx, vy, speed, spread …). Stepped and drawn by the wardrobe in a run, in
  the menu and on the bench, outside the event's rooms too: 'lit' kinds
  under the ship, 'glow' kinds in the additive pass.
  me.every(key, rate, fn): rate per second, the same at any frame rate,
  never more than 24 at once. Inside hlSkinTick only.
  The game's own sparks are dressed through hlSkinDebris instead.

ROPE, CLOTH, CHAIN, SPRING: made on first ask, stepped for you
  me.rope(key, sock, n, seg, o) → a THE KIT verlet rope, head pinned to the
      socket, streaming astern. Draw with hlDrawRope(b, { w0, w1, col }).
  me.cloth(key, sock, cols, rows, seg, o) → a THE KIT cloth, its pinned edge
      laid across the hull at the socket, rows trailing astern. Draw with
      hlDrawCloth(b, { col, dark, hi, lit }). o.spread widens the edge,
      o.rot turns it off the axis, o.squash; o.tear tears. Nothing of it is
      let forward of the pin line (o.behind, default on): spun half a turn,
      a cape drags round the stern rather than folding over the hull.
  me.chain(key, sock, n, seg, o) → THE KIT's hlChain rooted at the socket,
      pointing astern (a tail, a tongue of flame): draw its .pts.
  me.spring(key, v, k, d) → THE KIT's hlSpring: set .to in hlSkinTick, read
      .v anywhere.
  How they stream: pushed against the pilot's velocity (o.drag / look.drag,
  1.2), settling astern at rest (o.settle / look.settle, 60 px/s²), with a
  flutter (o.flutter, 40). They go when a run starts or the prize comes off,
  and are re-hung when the pilot changes.
  Ask for them in hlSkinTick or an Fx hook, never first from a draw hook.

ROUNDS: each of the pilot's rounds has a record, R
  R { b, seed, age, dist (px flown), x0, y0 (born), x, y, ang, crit, mine
  (the pilot's own, not a hacked one), kind 'plain' | 'crit' | 'venom' |
  'solar' | 'deep' | 'hacked', art }
  It is made the first time the wardrobe sees the round and dropped the
  frame it is gone (hlSkinRoundEndFx). me.rounds is the map.

TRAILS
  me.trailPts(sock, maxAge) → points newest first, from where the pilot is
  now: { x, y, age, k (0 now → 1 oldest), speed, dash }. With a socket, the
  points are where that socket was (me.trailPts('tipL'): a wingtip ribbon).
  me.ribbon(pts, o) (= hlWrRibbon): a filled ribbon through them.
    o { w0, w1 (width head → tail), a0, a1 (alpha), col, ease, comp
        ('lighter'), wob (noise on the width), wobSpd, seed, edge, edgeA, lw,
        quads (shade segment by segment: for a trail that curls back on
        itself) }

LIGHT
  me.lamp(x, y, r, col, a, flick, seed) (= hlWrLamp): the kit's light falloff
  laid on additively, with its candle flicker. It brightens and cannot
  darken: a light's look anywhere without a light pass (an ordinary run, the
  menu). Returns the flicker, so a flame can breathe with its own glow.
  me.light(o): a real light, inside hlSkinLights only.

SPRITES: expensive things made once
  hlWrSprite(key, w, h, draw(g, w, h), res = 2): its own canvas, origin at
  the centre, in the units it will be drawn in. hlWrBlit(key, x, y, ang,
  scale, alpha). hlWrUnsprite(key) (or all). Make them in hlSkinPreload.

  me.seed(obj) → 0..1, the same for the same round / body / moment every time
  hlWrStockHull(me, col) (hull space) and hlWrStockRim(me): the stock look,

  hlWrHookState() → [{ name, art }]: which hooks the art has drawn
  HL_WR.debug: the overlay

8. WHERE THE BUDGET GOES
================================================================================

The planning sheet puts the prizes in tier B. These are the places a prize
is looked at longest and where the extra budget pays back most:

  S  the equip (the menu stage plays it every time it is put on), the death
     (the last thing a run shows), the streak milestones, a boss kill (o.boss)
  A  the hull itself (hlSkinHull / HullSwap, hlSkinFront), the rounds (they
     are on screen more than anything else the pilot owns), kills
  B  the trail, dash and afterimages, level and wave, hurt and heal, low, the
     bodies' dressing, the screen pass

  The line is carved to burning, so each tier should keep what the tier
  below had, grown, the way the imperial line does: me.tier is the only
  thing that has to differ. The soul is its own thing (me.fx 'lostsoul').
