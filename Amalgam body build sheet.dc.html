This sheet documents the body as it is actually built in the awakening render — every number the renderer reads, so the chassis can be rebuilt, justified in fiction, or re-rendered for the rework fight specifically. It is not a description of the shipped Amalgam. Section 9 lists where it departs and what each departure needs a reason for.

1 · COORDINATE SYSTEM

x — right, world units (1u ≈ 1 game pixel at plan scale)
y — depth; south is +y, toward camera
z — up; 0 is the plating
origin — BOSS_X 0, BOSS_Y −900, chest centre in plan

Part offsets ox / oy are relative to that origin. Full span is 704u tall × 570u wide in plan — taller than wide, which is the proportion that keeps him reading as a body rather than a slab; the duel is fought across ~1500u of floor, which is what gives his output beam real length on screen.

3 · SILHOUETTE RULE — THE BOXES MUST OVERLAP

The single most important constraint on the chassis. If the nine footprints do not intersect, the body reads as shapes on sticks and the rig reads as scaffolding. Every adjacent pair overlaps on its shared axis, so the union of the boxes is one closed silhouette and the spars are internal structure that happens to be visible.

head ∩ spine  12u on y (neck)
spine ∩ torso  41u on y
shld ∩ torso  36u on x, 1u on y
shld ∩ spine  41u on y
arm ∩ shld  22u on x
leg ∩ torso  7u on y (hip)

The overlaps are deliberately small. An earlier pass overlapped far harder and the body read as sandwiched — 374u of head, neck and chest crammed into each other. The rule is the minimum intersection that closes the silhouette, and no more: the head sits 114u clear of the chest on a long neck, which is what gives him a head instead of a bulge.

4 · THE RIG

Eight spars. Drawn in three passes — #07050c at 26u, #191428 at 18u, then a #d8b4fe conduit at 3.5u — plus a dashed white pulse that travels toward the chest, so the read is nine donors feeding one aperture.

shldL→armL  ·  shldR→armR  ·  torso→legL  ·  torso→legR

5 · THE CHEST APERTURE

FORGEHEART is not decoration — it is the muzzle, and it must look like the part that makes the beam. The beam origin is (torso.x, torso.y + 10, torso.z), so it tracks the chest through the stand-up and the death throw automatically.

Aperture ring — r = 0.62 × core r (≈61u), stroke #f472b6
Collector vanes — 10, swept 0.3rad, #f9a8d4
Vane travel — opens 0.42 → 0.92 of ring r under load
Load — 0.25 idle, 0.55 + surge×0.45 while locked

6 · PLAN → STANDING

The same nine boxes serve both readings. A single stand scalar (0 → 1) rotates them out of the floor plane: plan-view layout becomes standing height, and it is driven by the meter, not by the clock — the frame breaks because the player earned it.

y = lerp(BOSS_Y + oy, BOSS_Y + oy×0.1, stand)
z = lerp(0, (188 − oy) × 1.02, stand)

Ground-contact ellipses are drawn per part at z = 0 before anything else, alpha falling off with height — they are what make standing read as height rather than as scale.

Each plate is a beveled box (bevel = 0.22 × min side), filled with a vertical PLATE_MD → PLATE_DK → #05040a gradient, seam lines at 1/plates intervals, one vertical seam at −0.18 w, and a lit top bevel. The donor tint washes over the plate at 0.10 alpha, rising to 0.40 as the body comes apart. Donor name is stencilled at the plate foot.

8 · DEATH THROW

Each part carries a fixed random unit vector (bx, by, bz) assigned at load, with bz biased upward (−0.2 … 1.4). On impact the parts travel b × easeOut(t/1.6) × (260, 230, 300) with a −t²×90 gravity term on z, and spin at t × 1.4 × bx. Because the offsets are fixed per part, the break-up is deterministic — the same silhouette comes apart the same way every take.

9 · DIVERGENCES FROM THE SHIPPED AMALGAM

These are the departures this render makes. Each one either needs a fiction for the rework fight or needs reverting before the render is treated as canon.

DEPARTURE NEEDS
Stands up. The shipped boss is a plan-view layout; here a stand scalar rotates him into a standing body for the back half. A reason the rig extends — pressure, damage, or the awakening itself provoking it.
Chest is the muzzle. Output comes from one FORGEHEART aperture with collector vanes, not from distributed emitters. Consolidating the rework's output into one aperture — or re-render with the real emitter set.
Plated mass. Parts are beveled plate boxes with seams and stencils, sized to overlap into a silhouette. Nothing in fiction — but the footprints must match whatever the rework's real part shapes are.
Donor names on the plates. Nine trophies, legible. A call on whether the Amalgam is labelled in-world or this is UI-only.
Boss is 900u upfield. Moved back so the standoff and his output column have length on screen. Arena geometry that actually affords ~1500u of duel floor.
Hard standoff wall. His output forbids closing inside 640u until the awakening collapses it — see §10. Confirmation this is the rework's intended defence, and a number for the real one.

10 · THE STANDOFF

His defining defence in this render: you cannot close on him by flying at him. Output pressure is a wall at 640u. Critically it does not shove — an impulse throws the player out of the fight and takes the beam off screen with them. It gives ground back at a rate, in clicks per second, and inside the wall that rate never falls below one. There is always pressure ticking, and it is always readable as a number.

CLICK = 20u  ·  CLICK_MX = 7 clk/s at the face of the wall
depth = clamp((wall − gap) / (wall × 0.55), 0, 1)
rate  = 1 + depth² × (CLICK_MX − 1)   // clk/s, never below 1
wall  = STANDOFF × (1 − walk)   // the awakening collapses it
Player approach — 420u/s under own power
Wall give-back — 20u/s at the edge → 140u/s at the face
Equilibrium — where the two meet is where you get to stand
Surge inside the wall — breaks lock, −22 heat, feed to zero

The surge no longer carries a velocity kick. It costs you the meter and the pressure you had built, and you give ground through the normal retreat target — nothing is ever launched.
