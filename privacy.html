VOIDRUNNER · privacy

Last updated 2 October 2026

VOIDRUNNER is a free browser game. It has no accounts, and it never asks for your name, email address or age. This page lists what it does keep, where, and who else is involved in running it.

On your device

  • Your save: your callsign, progress, unlocks and settings, plus a random profile id the game makes up the first time you play. It is kept in your browser's storage for this site.
  • A save belongs to the address it was made on, and clearing this site's data in your browser deletes it.

On the game's server

  • Leaderboards: the callsign you play under, your score and the run's stats, the time it was set, and your profile id. Callsigns and scores are public. Profile ids are never shown to anyone.
  • Events and rewards: which event quests a profile id has completed, which reward skins a profile id holds, and which old callsign a profile claimed back after the move.
  • IP addresses, briefly, to stop abuse: how many new event profiles an address made today (kept for the day), failed developer-login attempts (fifteen minutes), and how often an address asked for a co-op relay (about three hours).
  • Co-op: to connect two players, each side's connection details, including network addresses, are passed through the server and deleted within an hour. During co-op, game data goes straight between the two players, or through Cloudflare's relay when a direct connection is not possible.
  • Request logs: the hosting service keeps routine logs of requests to the game's server for a few days.

Other services the game uses

  • Cloudflare hosts the game and its server.
  • Google Fonts supplies the game's fonts, and esm.sh supplies a code library it loads. Both see your browser's request for those files.
  • Google AdSense shows an occasional banner on the main menu, never during play. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve and measure ads. How Google uses this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can turn off personalized ads at adssettings.google.com.
