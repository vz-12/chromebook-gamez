Frequently asked questions

Answers for new players

Quick answers about VOIDRUNNER, a free browser roguelite arena shooter.

Is VOIDRUNNER free?

Yes. There is nothing to buy to play the whole game. It is supported by an occasional banner on the main menu (never during a run) and by optional Patreon supporters, who unlock cosmetic looks.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No. It runs in the browser. If you want an app-style icon, most browsers let you install it from the address bar or menu.

What devices does it work on?

Laptops and Chromebooks with keyboard and mouse, phones and tablets with touch controls, and gamepads. See the controls guide.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your save is kept in your browser's storage for the address you play on, so clearing this site's data deletes it. An optional account (a name and a password, no email) keeps a copy of your save so it follows you to other devices.

It is blocked on my school network. What can I do?

Some filters block the .online domain. Try voidrunner.play101.workers.dev, which most filters allow. Both addresses have the full game, leaderboard, accounts and co-op. Saves are separate between the two addresses unless you sign in to an account.

How does co-op work?

Two players connect over the internet and fly together. When a direct connection is not possible, game data goes through Cloudflare's relay. Details are on the privacy page.

How do I get the other pilots?

Two are available from the start; THE HACKER and THE VAGRANT are unlocked by in-game goals. See the pilot guide.

How do the leaderboards work?

Every player's best score is kept on public boards, and you can look up any callsign on the leaderboard page. Profile ids are never shown.

What information does the game collect?

No real name, email or age. Everything it keeps, and why, is listed on the privacy page.
