The pilots

Four ships, four ways to play

Before each run you choose a pilot. Every pilot has its own weapon, health and speed, and they reward different habits. Two are available from the start; the other two are earned by doing something difficult in the game.

VOIDRUNNER

Rapid bullets, even footing. The default pilot: fast, forgiving, and effective at any range. If you are new, start here. Its steady stream of bullets lets you learn the arena, the enemies and the upgrade cards without a gimmick to manage. Health 100.

EMBER

A point-blank lance that flies. EMBER fires a lance that pierces everything in its line, and it burns hottest at the muzzle: the far tip does about half the damage. That rewards getting close, which is dangerous, and EMBER is faster and more fragile than the default ship (health 88). EMBER also flies, so bodies and wreckage do not block its lance. A higher fire rate shortens the charge rather than adding extra shots, so choose cards with that in mind.

THE HACKER

Weak fire, turns the room. THE HACKER is barely armed, because the gun is not the weapon. Whatever it kills stands back up on your side and keeps fighting until something puts it down again: up to eight at once, plus one boss. Playing it means thinking about which enemies are worth killing first. To unlock: carry every malware at least once.

THE VAGRANT

No gun. The reach of an arm. A melee pilot in a game about keeping your distance. It lives on the parry: every parry in a row raises its damage, and a perfect parry sends the blow straight back as a narrow wave. It has the most health of any pilot (124) and is the fastest, but it does not level into a build through cards; everything it becomes comes from the blades, plates, cores and sigils that the room leaves on the floor. To unlock: top the boss rush with a very high score.

Choosing

  • Learning the game: VOIDRUNNER.
  • Playing risky for big damage: EMBER.
  • Prefer tactics to aiming: THE HACKER.
  • Want a challenge that plays like a different game: THE VAGRANT.
