OVERDRIVE · the evolution

The ignite, and the form it takes

The stock beat with a short vent: the room dims, something comes for EMBER, it lands, the room catches, the shell opens and the lance turns. Drag the bar to hold any moment of the cut-in.

the vent fires
The swarm
In place of the falling spark. Forty fire beetles stream in from every edge on curling flights and land on the shell one by one, each lighting the spot it hits. The last lands exactly when the spark did, and the room catches.
The room
The stock pools along the walls and across the floor, burning blue with violet edges and cold white coals.
The form
The elytra fling wide and stay up. Two wings of blue fire unfold from under them, a white core burns in the gap, the seams light, and every plate of the body carries a crest of flame.
The lance
The helix's two strands become two braided jets, cyan in front and violet behind, flaring where they cross, with the flame running inside them.

OVERDRIVE · V · fusion core

Khepri, and the sun it rolls

Where the stock V makes EMBER into IFRIT cupping a sun, the beetle becomes Khepri, the scarab that rolls the sun across the sky. Same timings, the same aura at exactly 120, the same suns thrown at the same rate; shortened here to a few seconds so it loops.

Khepri
Lit from the front by the sun it holds: a broad domed shell split by its seam, the head-shield's rake of six teeth, toothed forelegs cradling the sun, the other legs braced at its sides, plasma wings half spread, and the body behind melted to a lashing tail of blue fire.
The throw
One foreleg flicks, then the other, and the held sun dims and swells back.
The suns
Each is rolled along by a small scarab on its back. They swell round what they take in, heat white as the fuse runs, pinch, and go up in a fireball and a shock ring, scarab and all.
The aura
A blue sun's surface at exactly what burns: convection turning inside it, prominences looping off the rim.
The screen

The heat, the prompts and the bursts

The last of the stock orange. Every number and rule is the stock HUD's own, so it reads exactly as it always did; only the look moves onto the beetle.

The heat
The bar is a run of body plates, cold at the empty end and white-hot at the full one, with the fire's head glowing where the heat has got to. Below the burn it stays slate, as the stock one does.
The keys
Little shells: a sheen across them and the seam's two ends notched in. Z burns down while it sprays; V holds a small sun while KHEPRI is out.
The words
VENT READY and the kit's damage numbers go to the light end of the ramp, so they still read on the floor.
Only this machine
Sparks, rings and waves are each player's own, so a partner sees their stock EMBER and nothing reaches the simulation.
