How to play VOIDRUNNER

A beginner's guide to the arena

VOIDRUNNER is a free roguelite arena shooter that runs in your browser. You fly a small ship around a closed arena, shoot waves of enemies, collect experience, and choose upgrades as you level up. Every run is different, because the upgrades you are offered and the order you take them build a different ship each time. When your health runs out the run ends, your score is recorded, and you start again, a little better at it.

Controls

Keyboard and mouse

  • W A S D: fly the ship.
  • Mouse: aim. Hold the button to fire.
  • Space: dash. A dash gives you a brief moment of invulnerability, so it is as much a defensive tool as a way to move.
  • P: pause. X: open the codex. S on the menu: settings.

Touch

  • Drag on the left half of the screen to fly.
  • The right half aims and fires; buttons on the right cover the other actions. The arena is wide, so the game asks you to hold a phone sideways.

Gamepad

  • Left stick moves, right stick aims, the right trigger fires, A dashes, B uses the pilot's special, and Start pauses.

How a run works

Enemies arrive in waves. Defeating them drops experience; filling the bar levels you up and offers a choice of upgrade cards. Cards change how your weapon behaves, how much damage you take, how fast you fly, and more, and good runs come from cards that work well together rather than from any single pick. Every few waves a boss arrives. Bosses have their own attack patterns, and learning those patterns is how you get further than you did last time.

The game keeps your best scores. Scores go to a public leaderboard, where every board can be searched by callsign.

Tips for your first runs

  • Keep moving. Standing still is how most early runs end. Circle the arena and shoot as you go.
  • Dash through danger, not away from it. The invulnerable moment of a dash can carry you straight through a bullet ring if you time it as the bullets reach you.
  • Read the telegraph. Boss attacks that would hurt most, such as ground slams, are marked on the floor a moment before they land.
  • Pick a direction with your cards. Two or three upgrades that reinforce one idea beat a spread of unrelated ones.
  • Try other pilots. Each of them plays differently; see the pilot guide.

Modes

  • Standard run: the main game: survive, beat the bosses, chase a score.
  • Daily run: a challenge that is the same for everyone that day, with its own leaderboard.
  • Boss rush: only the bosses, one after another. Your score rewards how deep you get and how fast.
  • Challenges: hand-built rule sets that change how a run plays.
  • Co-op: two players fly together over the internet, with no download needed.

Accounts are optional. Without one, your progress is kept in your browser; with one, it follows you between devices. See the privacy page for exactly what is kept.
