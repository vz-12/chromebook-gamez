0. AT A GLANCE
================================================================================

  WHAT IT IS
    The yearly October event. Each year is a BOOK: the same place, the same
    guide and the same systems, with one new chapter of the storyline on top.
    This is Book I. The Outsider (the evolution-tutorial guide) knocks from
    under the menu, leads the player down to a new hub, THE VIGIL, where a
    LOST SOUL waits. The soul sends the player up into three areas, one after
    another, to kill three bosses and bring back the pieces of itself. Each
    quest lights candles on a worldwide counter (prizes for everyone who took
    part) and pays a medal (two worn at once, run modifiers for the month).

3. THE STORY
================================================================================

  THE PREMISE, from one fact the game already has
    Quitting a run from the menu files it: quitToMenu() calls gameOver(), and
    it gets a score, a place on the board and a line in the ledger. Closing
    the tab mid-run does none of that. The run is never written down. It
    stops where it was, and it stays there.
    A LOST SOUL is one of those runs: a tab closed, and nothing came for it.

  THE THEME: things that never end properly. Each boss is a person, in the
  game's usual way, stuck on not finishing:
    THE BACKLOG     never finishes what he has   "I'll get to it."
    DEAD GAME       never leaves                 "Still here, though."
    THE RAGE QUIT   never lets anything end      "That one didn't count."
  The soul is what they leave behind; the event is the soul getting finished.

  THE HALLOWEEN TIE: the jack-o'-lantern legend is itself a lost-soul story
  (Stingy Jack, refused everywhere, wandering with a coal in a carved turnip).
  So the soul carries a carved lantern once it has one, the prize skins are
  lanterns, and the pumpkins belong to the story rather than decorating it.

  THE PEOPLE
    THE OUTSIDER (existing). The guide. Flat, singular, unconditional; tells
      you what a thing costs, never that you are welcome. He left the crowd
      himself, which matters by the end: the soul was LEFT, and he LEFT. He is
      the one thing that does not change over the event.
    THE LOST SOUL (new). A run whose tab was closed at WAVE 19, HULL 4%, in
      the ordinary starting hull. It does not know who it flew for, only that
      "he said he'd be right back."
      Voice, by how much of it has come back (HL_STEADY):
        0 start       fragments, lowercase, trailing: "...nineteen."
        1 after Q1    lowercase, still uneven
        2 after I     lowercase, whole sentences, steadier (0.35)
        3 after II    capitals, full sentences, its own voice (0.7)
        4 after III   plain and sure (1.0)
        5 the end     gone
      Look, by state (the user draws it; all six are in hl-art.js):
        0  a small cold light on the floor, beside one unlit candle
        1  a hull outline gathers round it, flickering at 4%
        2  it holds a carved lantern
        3  the hull is whole, still cold
        4  the lantern burns warm
        5  gone; its candle stays lit
    THE BOOKKEEPER (existing; optional cameo) [OPEN]. Unfiled runs are the one
      thing not in his ledger. One dry line at most, e.g. in the codex: "Not
      my department. Somebody ought to have made it one."

  THE QUEST RUNNER (built, general)
    hlCurrent(): the first quest not done, if its chapter is open and it is
    built (q.later marks the unbuilt ones; the chain stops in front of the
    first and the soul says the "wait" line).
    Progress is kept per profile (prog), so an errand's pieces survive a death.
    A quest finished up in an area is TOLD in the vigil: the soul only changes
    once you walk back to it (hlShown). A boss quest's telling starts with the
    soul rising a landing (HL_ASC_T 2.8 s).

  HOW AN AREA PLAYS (the frame is built; THE PATCH uses it)
    - Entered through its way up in the vigil ([E]), with the selected pilot.
    - Runs as a pseudo-challenge (Chal.on = { hl: id }): five waves of the
      area's roster, the boss on the fifth.
    - Scaled as a slice of an ordinary run (waveFrom):
        THE PATCH  waves 6–10 · THE HOUSE 11–15 · THE YARD 16–20
        (THE YARD ends where the soul ended.)
    - HEAD START: you go in with what the earlier waves would have paid, in
      full (rushSkipped over waves 1..waveFrom−1: XP and credits), so the
      level-up cards are picked at the door. THE VAGRANT gets gear instead.
    - The boss comes on wave 5 only once that chapter's errand is done. If it
      is not, wave 5 ends the run gently: "THE PATCH LETS YOU GO". A strong
      player does a chapter in one run; anyone else in two.
    - Ending: win or let go → HL_END_T (4.2 s) → back to the vigil.
      Death → the death screen ("WHAT YOU FOUND IS KEPT"): SPACE runs it again,
      M goes back down to the vigil.
    - Solo only for now (an area refuses to start while a co-op link is up).
      Co-op later [OPEN]: new enemy types sync through ETYPE on their own.
    - Medals apply inside areas.

6. CHAPTER I — THE PATCH [BUILT]
================================================================================

  A field under a low orange light. Every gourd in it is a run somebody
  paused and never opened again. THE BACKLOG farms them.
  Stage: bg #08060a, grid #1a120a / #2b1d0e, wall #7c2d12, accent #fb923c.
  waveFrom 6. Roster weights: jack 40 · gourd 34 · creeper 28 · scarecrow 24.

  6.1 AREA RULE — RIPENING, AND THE PILE (reworked 27 Sep)
    - A gourd carves its face over GOURD_RIPEN (15 s) up to GOURD_LIT (0.85)
      and then HOLDS, lit. It never holds a wave: a wave can end with gourds
      standing. That is the pile.
    - Every unbroken gourd makes everything else 3% faster (PILE_STEP). The
      HUD shows it (drawHlPileHud): THE PILE · n · +x%.
    - When the next wave starts, every lit gourd comes open (HL_HATCH_T 1.2 s)
      as a JACK.
    - When THE BACKLOG arrives, every gourd still standing is his: each slides
      to him (HL_REEL_SPD 420 px/s, staggered from 0.7 s) and is eaten on
      arrival: +5% of his base health (HEAP_HP) and a thicker heap. They are
      still gourds while they slide: break one and he never gets it.
    - So waves 1–4 are the preparation for the fight: break them and he
      arrives smaller.

  6.2 THE ENEMIES (base stats; scaled like any wave)
    GOURD      r16 hp26 spd0 dmg0 · 2xp. Does not move or attack. Ripens as
               above. The soul's COLD gourds are different (6.3).
    JACK       r17 hp44 spd64 dmg12 · 4xp. Keeps JACK_WANT (220 px), lobs seeds
               (every 2.0–2.8 s within 640 px). Up close (JACK_BREATH_R 130 +
               40) it winds up JACK_WIND (0.5 s, still turning to you) and
               breathes down a locked heading for JACK_BREATH_T (0.8 s), half-
               width JACK_BREATH_A (0.55 rad), 0.6× dmg per 0.2 s tick; 3.2 s
               before the next. THE FACE (±JACK_FACE 0.7 rad): hits there do
               double, and the first one puts the candle out (hlDark): no more
               breath.
    CREEPER    r15 hp34 spd70 dmg8 · 4xp. Keeps 220–320 px, throws a vine
               (VINE_SPD 520, VINE_RANGE 380, every 2.6–4 s). Held: 40% slower
               (VINE_SLOW 0.6), reeled in at VINE_REEL 150 px/s, for up to
               VINE_HOLD 4 s. Cut it by fire anywhere but the last VINE_SPARE
               (40 px) at the hull, or dash through it.
    SCARECROW  r18 hp60 spd150 dmg20 · 5xp. Perfectly still while your aim is
               on it (±CROW_WATCH 0.33 rad, within CROW_RANGE 1100); fast when
               you look away. Contact hurts (~26 at area scale).
    Under THE HACKER (the user's army rework): a taken jack's breath hits its
    foes (hlJackBreathTurned); a taken creeper's vine holds enemies
    (hlVineCatch) and your fire passes it (fixed 27 Sep; ported 28 Sep); a
    taken scarecrow never freezes under your aim.

  6.5 THE BACKLOG — who he is
    The hoarder: buys everything, finishes nothing, believes one day he will
    get to all of it, and that belief is the whole problem. A heap that walks:
    gourds, sealed boxes and a price sticker on everything.
    Base: r64, hpMul 1.5, spd 40, colour #fb923c. Secret (kept out of the
    codex count, freeplay spawns and the skin editor).
    Lines (world barks):
      entrance "Oh — careful. Those are mine."
      idle     "I'll get to it." · "It was on sale." · "Not now. Later." ·
               "I'm saving that one."   (every 7–11 s)
      a gourd  "Another one. For later."  (60% of the time)
      binge    "Fine. FINE. All of it. Now."
      death    "...I was going to play that one."

  6.6 HIS FIGHT — UNFINISHED
    Everything he starts, he abandons partway, and the abandoned thing waits
    exactly where it stopped: a PIECE in the pile (hlHeld).
    Every 1.5–2.1 s (−0.15 per binge spent) he starts one (volley 50%,
    sweep 25%, slam 25%); n = binges spent, 0–3, makes each bigger.
      VOLLEY  9+2n rounds fanned 0.11 rad apart at 250+20n px/s, 0.55× dmg.
              Paused 0.15–0.55 s after it is fired, in the air.
      SWEEP   a bar SWEEP_LEN 760 × SWEEP_W 16 from him, arc 1.6+0.2n rad,
              SWEEP_SPD 1.1+0.15n rad/s after a SWEEP_TELE (0.6 s) telegraph,
              0.9× dmg (0.5 s between hits). Paused at 35–80% of its arc.
      SLAM    a ring SLAM_R 110+10n where you stood, counting SLAM_T (1 s),
              1.3× dmg on landing. Paused at 30–90% of its count.
    FINISH IT FOR HIM (built 27 Sep). Fire passes through a paused piece.
    Going through it — the dash, THE VAGRANT's lunge or its swing — finishes
    it the way you were going, as YOURS (gold): it hurts him and his side and
    never you.
      volley  flung the way you went, spread kept, ×1.7 speed (KICK_SHOT),
              3 s life; each round 1.6% of him (TURN_SHOT)
      sweep   swings on round its pivot the side you pushed it from, 1.8 rad
              (SWEEP_KICK_ARC) at ×1.6 speed; 7% of him (TURN_SWEEP)
      slam    slides like a puck: 760 px/s (SLAM_KICK_SPD), friction 0.25/s,
              banks off walls, goes off on him or where it stops after
              SLAM_SLIDE_T (1.1 s, about 430 px); 10% of him (TURN_SLAM)
      anything of his in the way: half its own health (TURN_ADD), at least 20
      reach: the hull plus KICK_REACH (14 px); a dash covers ~184 px
      each finish: a short hitstop, a shake, a gold slash
    HIS HEAP. Anything else gets through at HEAP_TAKES 30%, −1% per gourd he
    has eaten (HEAP_STEP), down to HEAP_FLOOR 15%. His own pile is how he is
    really hurt.
    HE EATS. He walks to the nearest unbroken gourd within 520 px at 1.4×
    speed: +3% heal (BACKLOG_EAT_HEAL) and +4 flat armour (BACKLOG_EAT_ARMOR)
    each. Arrival pile: +5% of base health each instead of the heal.
    THE BINGE. At 75 / 50 / 25% health (BACKLOG_BINGE), and whenever nine
    pieces are paused at once (HL_PILE_MAX): everything still paused goes off
    the way it was going — at you, as RESUMED (red), never paused again. The
    pile is ammunition and a debt at once. He gets 0.9 s after a binge before
    he starts anything new.
    DISTANCE. Without a gourd to walk to, he keeps 210–330 px from you and
    circles (was 300–460; closer so he is on screen: you kick at him).

  6.7 HIS WAVE (reworked 28 Sep)
    A trickle, not a crowd: bossPool jacks 60 / gourds 40 (no creepers — a vine
    holds you still; no scarecrows — they want watching; his fight needs your
    dash and your eyes). HL_BOSS_ADDS 8 in all, HL_BOSS_LIVE 2 at once (plus
    him), one every HL_BOSS_TRICKLE 3.5 s, the first after 2 s. Gourds that
    come up in his wave are his food: the one he is walking to is marked.

  6.8 READING IT — the telegraphs (28 Sep: "the fight looks cluttered")
    One loud thing at a time, loudest first:
      1. what hurts you now        live and resumed pieces, adds' attacks
      2. what is about to          a binge winding up: red
      3. what you can do now       the piece a dash would finish: gold
      4. the rest of his pile      FURNITURE, faded to HL_QUIET_A (30%)
    - FADE: a paused piece not about to be finished is drawn at 30% (the logic
      scales every alpha the art sets around its draw call: hlQuietDraw; an
      art file that wants to do it itself declares HL_ART_QUIET).
    - THE DASH LINE: every frame the logic works out which paused pieces a
      dash pressed now would finish (the stick's way, or the nose), and where
      each would then go. Those light gold with chevrons (drawHlTele), and he
      gets brackets if that way finds him. Pieces merely in reach get a small
      diamond where a dash would meet them.
    - THE WIND-UP: a binge is CALLED, then comes BINGE_WARN (1.1 s) later. He
      says his line at the call and starts nothing new; the pieces shake and
      show in red where they will go; anything finished in that time is yours.
      With nothing paused, nothing is called.

                          (Not bosses, gourds or taken bodies.)
  ONE DAY        [BUILT]  XP from bodies is put aside instead of paid, then
                          paid all at once, ×HL_BANK_MUL (1.5), when a boss
                          dies. The bank shows on the medal HUD.
  SHEET          [DESIGNED] The dash keeps you intangible 0.35 s longer and
                          you pass through bodies while it lasts; it
                          recharges 25% slower.
  STILL HERE     [DESIGNED] Once a run, the killing blow leaves you a ghost for
                          8 s: untouchable, half damage. Kill 10 before it
                          ends and you stand back up at 30% hull; fewer and
                          the run ends.
  SHALLOW GRAVE  [DESIGNED] One body in ten you kill climbs back out on your
                          side for 8 s (THE HACKER's ally code). On THE
                          HACKER itself: its bodies last 25% longer instead.
  LAG            [DESIGNED] One enemy round in six that comes within reach
                          stutters and hangs in the air for a second.
  THE LANTERN    [DESIGNED] The soul's lantern rides at your shoulder: it shoots
                          what you shoot, weakly, and once a wave takes a hit
                          for you.

  THE BACKLOG's fight (a longer dash is a longer finish; LAG stutters rounds,
  paused ones included) — check them against Chapter I when they are built.
