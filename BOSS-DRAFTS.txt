VOIDRUNNER — BOSS DRAFTS
Two more finales, and the true ending.
Drafted 9 Sep 2026. Nothing here is built. Prepatch only; never deployed.


================================================================================
0. THE FRAME
================================================================================

This game is social commentary. Every finale is a PERSON, not a metaphor.
What exists:

  THE AMALGAM   the desperation of a creator, and the insatiable player who
                beats anything for content.
                "It is never 'that was good'. It is always 'is that all'."
                (see DEV_TALK / DEV_RAGE)

  ROOT          a bratty cheater exploiting the game's main vulnerability:
                the console.
                "it is not cheating if I wrote the rules."
                "you are only here because you looked it up."

  THE FOUNDER   a collective, and conformity. The crowd is the fight; you turn
                them by bleeding in front of them rather than taking the door
                he keeps open.
                "They were happy. You saw them."

Common thread: all three CARE TOO MUCH, and all three are stuck in here
because of it. That is what the last two and the finale are built on.

Adjacent, already in the game and worth not duplicating:
  THE SHOPKEEPER  turns up when a shop has been worked too hard — the pull,
                  the reroll, compulsion.
  THE UNWRITTEN   the thing with no entry; the four-day mechanic nobody
                  mentioned.
  THE BOOKKEEPER  the narrator. Dry, clerical, faintly exasperated. All codex
                  lines are in his voice.


================================================================================
1. BOSS FOUR — "SOLVED"
================================================================================
(alternates: THE OPTIMAL, BEST IN SLOT, THE META)

TARGET
  The optimiser. Never breaks a rule. Reads the game, finds the one correct
  answer, publishes it, and now everyone plays that. The tier list. The
  wiki-optimal. "You're having fun wrong." It believes it is HELPING, which is
  what makes it worse than ROOT.

VOICE
  Patronising, certain, warm.
  "You can play it that way. It is just worse."
  "I ran the numbers so you would not have to."
  "That is a fun build. Fun is a tier."

MECHANIC — it takes your choices away
  Each phase it strikes upgrades out of the offer pool. First the ones it has
  decided are bad, then the ones you actually like. Level-up shrinks
  3 cards -> 2 -> 1. The last card is always the same card. It has been the
  same card the whole time.
  It fights using exactly the build it recommends, played perfectly.

COUNTERPLAY (and the point)
  Beat it WITH the optimal build, on its terms — works, and it is the harder
  fight because it plays that build better than you.
  OR decline the last card. Take nothing. Refusing is the only input it has no
  line prepared for. That is the win the boss is about.

ROUTE IN
  Clearing most of the challenge ladder. Fifty runs of letting the game tell
  you how to play. Something notices you are good at being told.

REUSES
  chalFilterPool already filters offers (ban / only / rar). Build-mirroring
  already exists in THE UNWRITTEN.

RISK
  Removing options is un-fun unless it clearly reads as the ARGUMENT rather
  than a debuff. Never let the pool hit zero by accident; the last card must be
  guaranteed.


================================================================================
2. BOSS FIVE — "ONE POINT OH"
================================================================================
(alternates: THE ROLLBACK, 1.0)

TARGET
  The fandom that wants the thing frozen. Loves it sincerely — that is the
  trap. Not hostile, LOYAL, and it will strangle the game to keep it exactly as
  it was the day it arrived. The counterweight to SOLVED: one demands the
  future, this one forbids it.

VOICE
  Aggrieved, fond, unmovable.
  "This was better."
  "You changed it. Nobody asked you to change it."
  "Put it back."

MECHANIC — it reverts you, using your own changelog
  Each phase strips one shipped feature, newest first, and QUOTES THE ENTRY
  while doing it:
    "'Bullet glow.' You did not need that."     -> effect gone
    "'Skins.' It looked fine."                  -> hull snaps to default
    "'Daily runs.'"                             -> arena reverts to THE GRID
  Upgrade stacks roll back a version at a time. By the last phase you are
  flying the base ship with nothing on it — HOLLOW, imposed rather than chosen.

COUNTERPLAY
  Beat it with the ship it insists was perfect. It is RIGHT that the base ship
  can win. It is WRONG that it is better. Proving both at once is the fight.

ROUTE IN
  Hold THE REDACTION (the 1.x commemorative). The boss of nostalgia should only
  be reachable by someone who actually was there. Flag already exists.

REUSES
  CHANGELOG is already an ordered list with ids — a script waiting to be read
  aloud. FXO, skinRefresh, P.up, stage re-skin all already do the reverting.

RISK
  Stripping cosmetics mid-fight reads as a bug unless loudly diegetic — the
  quoted line must land BEFORE the revert. Must restore everything on exit,
  including on quit mid-fight.


================================================================================
3. THE FINALE — "THE NEXT THING"
================================================================================
(alternates: THE FEED, THE CHURN, RETENTION)

THESIS
  The five are all deformed love. Every one of them cares far too much, and
  every one is stuck in here because of it. The true final boss NEVER CARED AT
  ALL — the thing that consumes this game and forty others and remembers none
  of them. The scroll. The autoplay. The next tab.
  It does not attack. It CLOSES THE GAME DOWN AROUND YOU, politely, the way an
  audience drifts off.
  The five fight beside you not out of redemption — none of them get nicer —
  but because its victory means nobody is here at all.

  THE CORE IDEA: the only way to stop a thing that eats content is for the
  content to stop existing. They erase their own world. You survive because you
  were never part of it.

VOICE
  Never insults you. Worse than that.
  "You have been playing for two hours."
  "There is more."
  "You do not have to finish."
  "Nobody will ask you about this."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SEQUENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ACT 0 — THE TAKEOVER (~25s)
  Not a fight. The game gets monetised.
  In-page ad panels stack over the canvas: banners, an interstitial, a fake
  cookie bar, an autoplay tile whose mute button moves. They advertise THIS
  GAME'S OWN fictional products — a season pass, a founder's pack, THE NEXT
  THING itself. Each one dismissed spawns two.
  HARD RULE: DOM overlays only. Never window.open (blocked anyway, and a real
  popup is hostile). Advertising itself is funnier than imitating a real brand
  and sidesteps trademark + AV flagging entirely.

ACT 1 — THE BLACK (30s)
  Everything stops. Audio to room tone.
  MOST FRAGILE 30 SECONDS IN THE GAME. Three requirements:
    1. WALL CLOCK, NOT dt. requestAnimationFrame stops when the tab is hidden,
       and a black screen is exactly what makes someone tab away. Date.now() at
       entry, compare each frame.
    2. NEVER FULLY FEATURELESS. One anchor so nobody reloads — a single faint
       scanline, or the audio floor dropping in steps. Enough to say
       "deliberate", not enough to relieve it.
    3. Sound at the edge of hearing at ~22s, so the portal is heard before seen.

ACT 2 — THE COMMUNITY (~40s)
  A portal opens. Through it: the Founder's stands, every seat empty but one.
  The collective, alone. He does not fight and does not argue. He is just still
  there, because he was never able to leave.
  Then ROOT tears in — not through the portal, through the SIDE of it, because
  a door is for people who ask. First time its vandalism is on your side, and
  it hates that.
      "i'm not helping. i just don't want it closed."
  Then the dev's voice (no body — devtalk already works this way).
  Then SOLVED. Then ONE POINT OH.

ACT 3 — THE LIGHT (~10s)
  Five sources converge and THE ARENA IS BUILT OUT OF THEM — the floor drawn by
  their combined light rather than skinned in. The light is what finally shows
  you the boss, which has been in the room the whole time.

ACT 4 — THE FIGHT
  Standard boss structure, five allies via e.hacked (machinery already works).
  No health bar; damage does nothing and the game says so. The fight is
  survival while the five reach position.

ACT 5 — THE ERASURE
  They coalesce into one body and fire it. Not a weapon — a DELETION.
  A progress bar pins to the top of the screen:

      ERASING  ########........  47%

  As it fills the game leaves: hazards, particles, HUD, floor, walls. Colour
  drains to white. The five stand in it and are erased in turn — in the order
  they arrived, so the Founder is last, still sitting.
  Then a white page with YOU on it, alone, and nothing else. Hold it.

ACT 6 — OUT
  You were never part of it, so you are what is left over.
    1. The white page becomes a rendered new-tab page — search box, tiles.
    2. Beat.
    3. Fade to black, a monitor power-LED blinking amber, then out.
  The whole game was played from the perspective of an actual player, so it
  ends by turning their screen off.

  WHAT IS ACTUALLY POSSIBLE: cannot navigate to a real browser home page,
  cannot power off a real monitor, and window.close() only works on
  script-opened windows — it WILL fail here. All three must be rendered
  imitations inside the page. drawCrashScreen already rewrites document.body
  and sets document.title, so the technique is proven in this codebase.
  Rendering it ourselves means we control every pixel of the timing.

================================================================================
4. HARD CONSTRAINTS (all four will break it if ignored)
================================================================================

  rAF PAUSES ON HIDDEN TAB
      dt comes from requestAnimationFrame and stops dead when the tab is
      hidden. Every timed beat in the finale must use wall clock.

  POPUPS
      In-page DOM only. No window.open. No real brands. No fake OS,
      antivirus, "your files are being encrypted", or webcam/mic theatre.
      The existing Aw-Snap crash screen is on the right side of this line
      because it is self-evidently part of the bit and recoverable. Going
      further gets the domain flagged and makes the last thing a player feels
      FEAR — which would waste an ending whose whole point is warmth.

  window.close() WILL FAIL
      Only closes script-opened windows. Render the shutdown; never depend on
      actually closing the tab.

  DO NOT DELETE THE SAVE FOR REAL
      The erasure may look total. Do not touch the real store. Let the RELOAD
      bring the game back — you cannot kill it, it is on a server. That is the
      better ending anyway.
      (Tempting beat: THE NEXT THING appears to wipe the save, ONE POINT OH
      restores it. Only do this if the restore is instant and the real store is
      never touched.)

  ESC WORKS THROUGHOUT.
