About VOIDRUNNER

A free game, made to be played in a browser tab

VOIDRUNNER is a free neon roguelite arena shooter. It runs in any modern browser, on a laptop, a Chromebook, a phone or a tablet, with nothing to download or install. It is built as a single page and is designed to start quickly and run on modest hardware, because a lot of its players are on school-issued Chromebooks.

What is in it

  • A run-based arena shooter with upgrade cards, bosses and a record of everything you encounter, called the codex.
  • Several pilots, each with a different weapon and style; see the pilot guide.
  • A boss roster across multiple stages, and a boss rush mode.
  • Daily runs and challenges, each with its own board.
  • Two-player co-op over the internet.
  • A public leaderboard covering every board, searchable by callsign.
  • A soundtrack made for the game, and a first-flight tutorial for new players.

Playing on school networks

Some school filters block the .online domain. The game is also reachable at voidrunner.play101.workers.dev, which most filters allow. Everything, including the leaderboard, accounts and co-op, works at both addresses. Progress saved on one address stays on that address unless you sign in to an account, which carries it between them.

Accounts and privacy

You never have to give an email address, a real name or an age. An account is only a name and a password, and it is optional. The privacy page lists everything the game keeps and who else is involved in running it.

Supporting the game

VOIDRUNNER is free. It is paid for by a single occasional banner on the main menu (never during play) and by supporters on Patreon, who unlock cosmetic looks. Supporting is entirely optional and does not change how the game plays.
