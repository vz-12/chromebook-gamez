VOIDRUNNER — THE VAGRANT'S RITE
The rite's design and its art guide: every hook, what it is handed, and when.
Written 29 Sep 2026. Prepatch only; never deployed.


================================================================================
0. WHAT IS BUILT
================================================================================

THE VAGRANT's rite is in index.html and plays end to end. Its boss is THE
FOUNDER (user, 29 Sep: SOLVED and ONE POINT OH, the finales it could otherwise
have answered, are not built). RITES.melee has a `start`, so THE VAGRANT's
statue in the hub now answers, and waking it is granted by the rite exactly as
EMBER's and THE HACKER's are.

  BUILT      the rite: five sequences, his lines, the awakening in the middle
             of the last exchange, the card, and back to the menu
  NOT BUILT  the awakened kit. The moves are being designed one at a time
             (user, 29 Sep: "we'll go over the moves one by one"). Until they
             land, a woken VAGRANT flies exactly as it did before.

  The form's name is a working name. FR_FORM = 'CADENCE' is the only place it
  lives: the banner after the wake, and the card ("AWAKENING · CADENCE · THE
  VAGRANT"). Rename it there when the kit has a name.

Everything you would see is a placeholder: the game's own figures (founderFigure,
crowdFigure, halberdGlyph) and shapes and labels, enough to play it and read
its beat. That is this guide's job.


================================================================================
1. THE FIGHT
================================================================================

THE PAIRING. On the ordinary route he opens a door for you when you are hurt,
heals you four times, and the fight is refusing it. There is one figure in all
of that he never opens it for: the one standing outside it, unlit, at the same
size as him. That is this hull. It has never been let in anywhere, it lives on
refusing what it is handed, and it wears whatever the room leaves on the floor.
He is the one man in the game who has never been outside.

So there is no door here, no gifts and no healing, and none of the tribunal.
It is the camp behind the tear: his fire, his seat, his people round it, and
all of it turned on the one thing it will not have in.

ONE VERB. His people do everything together, on his count: whatever they
throw lands on his beat. That is what makes them his, and it is what makes them
answerable. A perfect parry on the beat catches all of it at once and sends
each throw straight back to the hand it came from, and whoever gets their own
throw back turns: looks at what they threw, and sits down with their back to
it. Nobody is struck. The blade only pushes his people, and it does not reach
him at all: a swing he is not open to is caught on the haft (half the chain,
and some ground). A refused blow of his own opens him, and the swing that
parry earned is the only one that lands.

HIS HEALTH is his hold on the room, 1000 of the rite's own units (FR_HP). One
of his people turned is 25 (all 24 of them are .6 of him); then it is him.
Nothing the build brings reaches it, as in ROOT's rite. The perfect parry's
stillness is off for the whole fight: this room keeps one time, and it is his.

THE ROOM. 1300 × 820. His fire in the middle (pure damage to stand in: you
cannot refuse a fire), his seat behind it, his people in a horseshoe round it
(10 near, 14 behind), open toward a door in the bottom wall. You come in at
the door. The camera holds the whole camp until he comes down, then the two of
you.

  THE ENTRANCE (5 s, input held)
    They sit round the fire with their backs to you.
      "Oh. Hello."
      "Ah. No. Not that one."      they stand and turn round to look at you
      "Everyone, please. On my count."

  1  HE KEEPS TIME            60 BPM · to .80 of him
     Seated, counting with the butt of the haft. Every other beat he raises
     one to three of the near ring; they throw on the next beat; it lands on
     the one after, on you. The first few beats he only counts.
       "We do things together here."

  2  EVERYONE UP              72 BPM · to .60
     The whole camp stands. Throws every beat. Every fourth beat he points at
     where you stand and three to seven of them throw at the floor there: stand
     in it and send all of it home, or step out and turn nobody. Now and then he
     HOLDS: the hands stay up a beat longer, nothing is in the air, and a guard
     raised on the beat they were due is raised at nothing ("Not yet.").
     He keeps eight back for the door, so a volley sent home all at once can
     never empty the room early.
       "Everyone up, please."

  3  SHOW IT OUT              80 BPM · to .40
     The door behind you opens on the cold. The eight still his come down to
     walk you through it: a step each beat, all together, longer from further
     off. One at your elbow raises its hands, rushes you (faster than you walk)
     and shoves on the next beat. A perfect parry throws it back and turns it; a
     plain one only throws it back; one that gets through pushes you at the
     door. Walk into the doorway, or be walked into it, and you are PUT OUT: 12%
     of your health, the chain, and two of the ones you turned sit back down
     (as ushers). Then they step back up the room and give you a moment. A
     crowd leaning on you slows you, down to 60%.
       "Would you show our friend out. Gently."

  4  HE COMES DOWN            84 BPM · to .15
     The halberd, on his beat. Every blow winds up for whole beats and lands
     ON one: SWEEP (1 beat), THRUST (1 beat; he goes with it), SLAM (2 beats,
     where you stood), SPIN (1 beat, then 3 landing beats). A perfect parry
     opens him: he reels into your reach for 1.6 s, and the swing it earned (a
     riposte, the finisher) lands for 40, more with the chain; any other blade
     while he is open lands for 12. One riposte and he is back. Anything of
     yours he is not open to is caught.
       ". . . fine. I will do it myself."

  5  THE LAST EXCHANGE        84 BPM, quicker each exchange
     The set piece, played. Face to face, framed, nothing else in the room.
     He strikes on one beat and you refuse it; your answer goes on the next
     (the hull swings it for you) and he catches it on the haft. Dead even.
     A blow that gets through costs 12% and is thrown again.
       "Sit. DOWN."
     On the seventh exchange the hull WAKES, in the middle of it, the way
     SUPERUSER comes in the middle of ROOT's race:
       "What. No. That is MY time."          the exchange holds 1.3 s
     From there the pace is the hull's (132 BPM), the guard is long and almost
     all of it perfect, and every answer lands. The haft breaks a piece at a
     time. On the eleventh it goes.
       ". . . close it on your way out."
     He is not killed and nothing reaches in to take him: he is disarmed in the
     middle of his own room. The card: AWAKENING · CADENCE · THE VAGRANT, "it
     was never going to sit down", and back to the menu.

  Waking is granted the moment it happens, not at the card.

NUMBERS (first drafts, all FR_* at the top of THE VAGRANT'S RITE)
  A beat's throws that get through: 6% of your health, +2% a throw past the
  first, capped at 16% (summing them made one missed volley a fifth of you).
  A shove 3% (+1.5% each more). His blows 9–12%. Put out 12% (pure). The fire
  4% a half second (pure). The perfect window is the game's own (0.09 s, and
  whatever the kit's `parry` makes of it); only in the exchange, after the
  wake, is it stretched (FR_WAKE_WIN / FR_WAKE_PERFECT).

3. THE BLOCK, AND THE RULES FOR ART CODE
================================================================================

The placeholders are one block in index.html, straight after THE VAGRANT'S
RITE's logic and before the portal room:

    /* ===========…
       THE VAGRANT'S RITE — art hooks                    (a line of its own)
    …
    /* ===================== end of THE VAGRANT'S RITE art hooks ===================== */

Lift the designed file over it whole, the way su-art.js and od-art.js were.
New helpers are fine: prefix them frArt* / FR_ART_*.

  - DRAW, DO NOT DECIDE. A hook may read `fr`, P and anything listed here and
    must not write any of it. Art-only fields go on the object with an `art`
    prefix (f.artSway), never an fr one.
  - Time comes from uiTime (always running) or the fields handed in (frBeatK(),
    frNextIn(), h.stT, r.t, th.p). Never accumulate dt inside a draw hook.
  - World hooks draw in world coordinates with the camera applied; screen
    hooks in CSS pixels (W × H).
  - Leave ctx as you found it (save/restore): source-over, alpha 1, textAlign
    left.
  - The …Fx hooks are called once, at the moment, from the logic. Spawn
    particles there (burst, ringFx, spawnPart) or start art-side timers.
    Returning anything does nothing.
  - The beat must stay readable over everything else. The ring on the hull
    (drawFrTells) is the whole fight: it closes to the hull's edge as the next
    beat arrives, and is gold for exactly the part of it a guard raised now
    would make perfect (frNextIn() <= bladePerfectWin()).
  - The same figures are there to use: founderFigure, crowdFigure,
    halberdGlyph, campScene, tearPath, and the ALL HALLOWS kit.

================================================================================
5. THE HOOKS
================================================================================

WORLD
  drawFrRoom(q)          every frame of the rite. q.room {x0,y0,x1,y1,cx,cy} is
                         where the walls are going (the arena animates in to
                         it); q.fire {x,y} (radius FR_FIRE_R 58); q.seat {x,y};
                         q.door {x,y,open 0..1} in the bottom wall, FR_DOOR_W
                         190 wide — it opens for SHOW IT OUT and HE COMES DOWN,
                         and the doorway is the last FR_DOOR_D (40) of floor
                         above it. Each person's place: f.hx, f.hy.
  drawFrFolk(q)          every frame. One per q.folk (see 6).
  drawFrHim(q, h)        every frame. h.st: 'seat' (counting: q.tap is 1 on
                         each beat and falls away), 'walk', 'ready', 'tell',
                         'live', 'rest', 'open', 'ex' (the exchange), 'down'
                         (disarmed). h.atk while winding up or striking.
  drawFrTells(q)         every frame. What is coming: q.due (next beat), q.marks,
                         h's wind-up. Everything a player times a guard by.
  drawFrThrows(q)        every frame, additive. q.throws and q.rets.
  drawFrWokenUnder(w)    from the wake through the card, under the hull.
  drawFrWokenOver(w)     …and over it. w: 0 at the wake, 1 once turned (1.3 s).
                         This is the woken hull's first look, before the kit
                         has one of its own.

SCREEN
  drawFrFrame(q)         under the HUD. The placeholder letterboxes the
                         exchange and the end; keep anything here off the
                         health bar's corner, or draw it lower.
  drawFrHud(q)           over the HUD. The count, his words (q.say while q.sayT
                         > 0), the hint (q.hint while q.hintT > 0), how many
                         are still his, the exchange's count.

FX (once each, logic side)
  frBeatFx(n)            every beat of his count (not the exchange's)
  frSeqFx(i)             a sequence begins (0..4)
  frThrowFx(th)          a throw leaves a hand
  frLandFx(n, parried, perfect)
                         a beat's blows land on you: n of them, whether a guard
                         was up, and how many it made perfect
  frMissFx(th)           a mark's throw lands on floor you had left
  frReturnFx(r)          a throw sent home, at the parry
  frTurnFx(f)            one of his people turns
  frShoveFx(f, res)      an usher's shove: res 'perfect' | 'parry' | 'hit' |
                         'miss' (you had gone)
  frPutOutFx()           you are put out (you are already back inside)
  frCaughtFx(x, y)       he catches a blade on the haft, at x, y
  frOpenFx(h)            a refused blow of his opens him
  frRiposteFx(h, dmg, fin)
                         the blade lands on him (fin: the riposte), or an
                         answer lands in the exchange
  frStrikeFx(h, move, hit)
                         his blow lands on the beat: move is h.atk, hit whether
                         it found you (before the guard answers it)
  frExClashFx(x, y, whose, res)
                         the exchange: whose 'his' (res 'parried' | 'hit') or
                         'yours' (res 'caught', before the wake)
  frWakeFx()             the hull wakes
  frSnapFx(h)            the haft goes


================================================================================
6. WHAT `fr` HOLDS
================================================================================

  fr.seq           0..4, the sequence (FR_SEQ[fr.seq].id: time, unison, out,
                   down, exch); fr.hold his hold, 0..1000; fr.turned
  fr.t             the rite's clock (it stops when the room is stopped)
  fr.bn, fr.bt, fr.per
                   his count: the last beat's number, time since it, seconds a
                   beat. frBeatK() is 0 on a beat and 1 at the next;
                   frNextIn() the seconds until it (the exchange's in the
                   exchange)
  fr.tap           1 on each of his beats, falling to 0
  fr.openT         > 0 during the entrance; fr.said which line of it is up
  fr.folk[]        his people:
                     x, y        where they are; hx, hy their place; a, ring
                     face        0 back to you, 1 facing you (turning in the
                                 entrance)
                     turned      not his any more (grey, back to you)
                     look        1 the moment they turn, falling to 0
                     st          'sit' | 'up' (standing) | 'usher' (coming for
                                 you) | 'home' (walking back to their place)
                     wind        the beat they throw on, or -1: a raised hand
                     mark        the mark they are throwing at, or null
                     shove       the beat an usher shoves on, or -1: hands up
                     busy        a throw of theirs in the air, or coming back
  fr.throws[]      in the air: f (who threw it), kind 'home' (at you) |
                   'mark' (at the floor), x, y (on the floor under it), h 0..1
                   (off the floor), p 0..1 (of its flight), land (the beat)
  fr.rets[]        going home: f, x, y, h, t 0..1
  fr.marks[]       where they all look: x, y, n (how many), land;
                   frInMark(m) is whether you are standing in it
  fr.due           the next beat: home, mark, shove (counts), hal (his move
                   or null)
  fr.him           x, y, ang; st and atk (see drawFrHim); left (beats left in
                   the state); mark (a slam's); openT (while open); stT (time
                   in the state); spinA (a spin's extra turn)
  fr.door          x, y, open 0..1
  fr.ex            the exchange: i (0..10), ph 'wind' | 'answer', bt/per, awake,
                   wakeT (> 0 while the wake holds it), introT, broke 0..1 (the
                   haft, after the wake), lost
  fr.end           the ending: t
  fr.say, fr.sayT, fr.hint, fr.hintT
  frWakeT          uiTime at the wake, or -1
