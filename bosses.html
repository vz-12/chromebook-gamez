The bosses

What you will face and how to survive it

A boss arrives at the end of each stage. Every boss has its own set of attacks and several phases, and its attacks get faster and denser as its health drops. None of them can be beaten by standing still, but all of them can be learned. This guide describes the bosses you meet in a normal run, in order.

Stage 1: THE WARDEN

The original. The Warden zones the arena with rings of bullets, fires spirals, and charges at you. It also fires its "ledger", ruled lines of shots that arrive over time. Keep circling: rings are easiest to avoid by moving across them rather than away, and a dash timed to the charge carries you through it.

Stage 2: FORGEHEART

An armoured boss. Rotating plates block your shots, so the damage happens in the gaps; position yourself so the gap lines up with your aim. It also hauls itself toward a slam point and summons small enemies from its body, so clear those before they box you in.

Stage 3: BROODMOTHER

She burrows, lays eggs and floods the floor with acid. While burrowed she cannot be hurt and moves quickly; she resurfaces on you. Her slam is telegraphed with a crater marker on the ground, so look at the floor and leave the circle.

Stage 4: THE ARCHIVIST

An evasive boss that blinks around the arena, sweeps lasers across it, and splits into decoys. Find the real one before spending your damage; stay clear of the laser sweeps by moving with them rather than against them.

Stage 5: NULL

The finale of the first part. NULL borrows tricks from every earlier boss and adds some: expanding bullet rings, converging boxes of bullets and aimed bursts. It has the largest health pool of the five, so the fight is a test of everything you have learned.

The Fracture: THE CURATOR

It files things away, including you. It keeps a record of what you have killed and puts it back, seals you inside a closing ring you have to shoot your way out of, and erases bands of the arena. It drifts to the arena centre and rules from there, so the edges are where you have room to move.

The Fracture: THE OTHER

The smallest and by far the fastest boss, and it spawns short-lived "virus" copies of itself that expire but still hurt. Do not chase it; hold a position with room to move and let it come to you.

General advice

  • Learn one attack at a time; each fight is a set of separate lessons.
  • Save your dash for the attack that you cannot walk around.
  • Build your cards before a boss, not during it. Bosses reward a build with a direction.
